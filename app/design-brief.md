# AKAL — Design Brief

## Design read
B2B marketing agency landing page for founders and marketing leaders who need a single accountable team for their full marketing operation. Register: confident, precise, calm authority — not hype, not agency slickness.

## Concept spine
**The operating system.** The page is a controlled interface where four capabilities (websites, creative, ads, optimisation) are shown as separate orbiting components that the scroll brings together into one unified system. The visitor scrolls the solution into existence.

## Delivery tier
`cinema` — scroll-scrub film as the Tier-1 hero mechanic, Lenis+GSAP for surrounding motion.

## Animation mode
`animated-website`

## Journey shape
`single-shot` — one continuous 15s film, scrubbed end to end.

## Journey (6 chapters)
The film: 4 translucent glass interface panels float separately in a dark void. As the scroll progresses they drift, orbit, and merge into one unified panel with the AKAL wordmark.

1. **Hero (0-2s)** — Four panels visible, separate, drifting in darkness. "Four capabilities."
2. **Websites (2-5s)** — The website panel (UI card with grid) drifts toward center. "Build the presence."
3. **Creative (5-8s)** — The creative panel (film frame) orbits in. "Shape the message."
4. **Ads (8-11s)** — The ads panel (dashboard) locks into place. "Turn on clarity."
5. **Optimisation (11-13s)** — The optimisation panel (chart) slides in and completes the set. "Improvement compounds."
6. **Closer (13-15s)** — All four panels merge into one unified glass surface with "AKAL" centered. "One accountable team, one operating system."

## World grammar
- **Perspective:** Static camera, centered, slightly low angle, looking at the floating panels in a deep void
- **Palette:** #0F1512 (deep green-black void), #B7E34A (neon lime accent), #C2CBCD (frosted glass white)
- **Light direction:** Soft key from upper-left, neon rim from right
- **Surface finish:** Frosted glass, subtle refraction, soft edge highlights, no hard noise
- **Background:** Dark gradient void with soft bokeh orbs
- **No on-screen text in the film** — all type is HTML

## Mobile framing
Keep the panel assembly in the center-safe area. Lighter mobile encode (720p cap).

## Locked palette
- **Background:** #0F1512 (deep green-black — dark, premium, technical)
- **Accent:** #B7E34A (neon lime — signal, growth, active)
- **Surface:** #C2CBCD (frosted glass — clean, premium, tactile)
- **Off-white ground:** #F5F3EE (warm cream — for reading sections)
- **Ink:** #1A1C1E (near-black text)
- **Muted:** #787C82 (secondary text)

## Locked type
- **Display:** Cabinet Grotesk (bold, clean geometric sans — confident, technical, premium)
- **Body:** Inter Tight (clean, readable, paired with the display)
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono (for data labels, metrics)

## Section plan
1. **Hero** — Scroll-scrub film as full-viewport cinematic. Overlay: headline "Four capabilities. One system." + sub "One accountable team running your entire marketing operation." + CTA "Request a growth plan"
2. **Services** — 2x2 asymmetric grid: 4 service cards with bespoke icons, each with headline + short description. No equal columns.
3. **How it works** — Simple 3-step horizontal process: Define → Align → Optimise. Each step has a short line.
4. **CTA** — Full-width, minimal. Huge headline "Ready to close the gaps?" + email field + CTA.

## Asset plan
- **Hero visual:** 15s scroll-scrub film (single-shot, 4 panels merging)
- **Section plates:** Generated brand imagery for each service card
- **Content imagery:** Abstract glassmorphic textures for backgrounds
- **Custom icon set:** 4 service icons in neon-lime stroke style
- **Logo:** "AKAL" wordmark in Cabinet Grotesk bold
- **OG:** Branded cover with stadium capsule

## CTA inventory
- **Primary CTA** (hero + footer): "Request a growth plan" — solid neon-lime button, rounded, on-hover the button fills with a darker green and the text shifts to white
- **Secondary CTA** (services section): "Learn more" — ghost link with an underline that draws on hover
- **Form submit** (CTA section): "Send" — same as primary but narrower
